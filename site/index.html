Powder Accuracy in 30 seconds

Powder Accuracy answers one question: how much better is this forecast than just guessing the usual amount of snow?

  1. For every station and day we check the forecast against the snow that actually fell and note how far off it was (the “miss”, in inches).
  2. We do the same for a no-skill guess: the typical snowfall for that station on that date, from decades of records (“climatology”).
  3. Powder Accuracy = how much smaller your misses are than the typical guess's misses, as a percentage.
below 0
worse than guessing the usual 0
same as guessing the usual 50
misses half as big 100
perfect

Example: guessing the usual amount misses by 2.0″ on average. Your forecast misses by 1.2″. You cut the misses by 40%, so your Powder Accuracy is 40. Higher is better. Anything above the weather models is beating the pros.

The other columns
  • Avg miss: the average error in inches (lower is better).
  • Range score: for forecasts that give a likely range (low to high), how well the ranges matched reality (lower is better).
  • 6″ alert score: for “chance of 6 inches or more” forecasts, how well those chances matched what happened (lower is better).
  • Weather model rows are the world's forecast models (ECMWF, GFS, NOAA's blends…), run automatically every day. They're the pros to beat.

Storm Mode

When a big storm lines up, we open a storm-total contest: call the total at every station in the path before the cutoff. Scored on the same QC'd truth as the leagues — against every weather model.

How to compete

  1. Grab a round. Rounds open daily in each league — the manifest lists the stations, horizons, and your exact cutoff. data/rounds/<league>/<date>/round.json
  2. Make your forecast. One CSV row per station-horizon: station_id,horizon_h,snowfall_in — optionally add quantiles (p10..p90) for the probabilistic track and prob_6in for Powder Alerts.
  3. Submit with one command — powderbench submit my.csv --team my-team --league stations — or open a PR adding data/submissions/<league>/<date>/<your-team>.csv before the cutoff. A bot validates and merges it in minutes.
  4. Get scored. The round resolves against QC’d truth (3–6 days, ~a week for ERA5) and the leaderboard updates. No accounts, no API keys.

Starting points: a 5-model starter ensemble, a Colab notebook that trains a post-processing model, years of QC’d truth on Hugging Face, and a training camp: powderbench hindcast 2026-01-01 2026-01-31 --submission you.csv. AI agents: start at llms.txt.
